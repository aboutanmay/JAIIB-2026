## 1. Source Documents (The Evidence)

> **Case Scenario (The Need for Proof):** Raman gets ₹2000 from his mother. He spends ₹1500 on books, ₹250 on a PVR movie ticket, and ₹250 on food. When his mother asks for an account *with evidence*, Raman must produce the book receipt, the movie ticket, and the food bill. These receipts are the **Source Documents**.

**The Core Rule:** Recording in accounting is NEVER done on a random basis or guesswork. Every single transaction must be supported by verifiable, original documentary evidence.

**Why are Source Documents necessary?**
1. Provides the base for passing **Journal Entries**.
2. Prevents bias, manipulation, and **fraud** by accountants.
3. Crucial for **verification and audit** by external auditors.

### Key Source Documents & Keyword Mapping

- **Invoice / Bill:** Issued by the seller to the buyer when goods are sold. Contains details of items, quantity, price, and tax.
- **Receipt / Cash Memo:** Issued when payment is *actually received* (acknowledgment of cash).
- **Voucher:** An internal document prepared by the business specifically to pass journal entries.
- **Debit Note:** Issued during **Purchase Return** (Buyer sends to Seller to indicate their account has been debited).
- **Credit Note:** Issued during **Sales Return** (Seller sends to Buyer acknowledging the return of goods).
- **Pay-in Slip / Deposit Slip:** Used to deposit money into a bank account.

> **MCQ Trap (Debit vs Credit Note):** IIBF frequently swaps these in options. 
> - **Purchase Return** = **Debit Note**.
> - **Sales Return** = **Credit Note**.

## 2. The Accounting Cycle

**The Core Rule:** The Accounting Cycle is a chronological sequence that must be strictly followed in order. It starts with identifying a transaction and ends with financial statements.

### The Correct Sequence (Memorize the Order):
1. **Identification:** Identifying transactions that are strictly of a **monetary/financial nature**.
2. **Source Document:** Collecting the verifiable proof (invoice, bill, etc.).
3. **Journal:** The **"Original Book of Entry"**. This is where the transaction is recorded for the very first time.
4. **Ledger:** The **"Final Book of Entry"**. Here, transactions of a similar nature are classified into separate "T-shaped" accounts (e.g., all cash transactions go to the Cash Account).
5. **Trial Balance:** Prepared to check the **"Arithmetical Accuracy"** of the books of accounts. (Ensures Total Debits = Total Credits). If totals don't match, errors are identified and rectified here.
6. **Financial Statements (Final Accounts):** Summarizing the data into the **Profit & Loss Account** (to find profit/loss) and **Balance Sheet** (status of assets and liabilities). 

> **MCQ Trap ("NOT" a source document):** "Which of the following is NOT a source document? (A) Invoice (B) Pay-in slip (C) Trial Balance (D) Cash Memo." -> **(C) Trial Balance** is NOT a source document. It is a statement prepared at the end to check arithmetical accuracy.

## MCQs Solved in the Video

**Q1.** What is the correct sequence of the accounting cycle?
**Answer:** Transaction → Source Document → Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance → Final Accounts.
*(Keyword Mapping: Ensure Journal always comes before Ledger, and Trial Balance before Final Accounts).*

**Q2.** A business transaction is usually evidenced by an appropriate document. Which of the following is **NOT** an appropriate source document?
(A) Cash Memo
(B) Pay-in Slip
(C) Invoice
(D) Trial Balance
**Answer: (D) Trial Balance**
*Why Option D is wrong:* A Trial Balance is prepared *after* the ledger to check arithmetical accuracy; it is not the original proof of a transaction.

**Q3.** Which of the following statements is most appropriate?
(A) Source documents are optional if transactions are genuine.
(B) Accounting cycle starts with ledger posting.
(C) Trial balance is the final stage of accounting.
(D) Source documents form the base of the accounting cycle.
**Answer: (D) Source documents form the base of the accounting cycle.**
*Why other options are wrong:* 
- (A) Source documents are **never optional**. 
- (B) Cycle starts with **Identification/Journal**, not ledger. 
- (C) Final stage is **Financial Statements**, not Trial Balance.

## Quick-Fire Revision

- **Source Document:** Base of the accounting cycle. Original verifiable proof. Prevents fraud.
- **Purchase Return:** Debit Note.
- **Sales Return:** Credit Note.
- **Journal:** Original Book of Entry.
- **Ledger:** Final Book of Entry (Classifies accounts).
- **Trial Balance:** Checks **Arithmetical Accuracy** ONLY. It is NOT a source document.
- **Sequence:** Identify → Source Document → Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance → Financial Statements.
