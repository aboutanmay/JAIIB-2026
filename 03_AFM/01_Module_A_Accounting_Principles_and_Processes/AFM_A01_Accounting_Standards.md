## Why Do We Need Accounting Standards?

**The Sonu vs Monu Example**
- Sonu and Monu both report ₹5 lakh revenue.
- **Sonu** correctly charged depreciation and recognized revenue only on actual sales.
- **Monu** delayed expenses, didn't charge depreciation, and recorded revenue in advance.
- You cannot compare them because they didn't follow the same rules (apples-to-oranges comparison).

**Accounting Standards** are the grammar and rules of the accounting language. They provide a common set of rules, principles, and procedures for preparing and disclosing financial statements.

**Objectives / Benefits of Accounting Standards:**
- **Uniformity & Comparability:** Ensures financial statements of different companies can be compared on the same base.
- **Reliability:** Makes statements trustworthy for investors and the public.
- **True and Fair View:** Presents the real picture of the business.
- **Reduces Frauds:** Eliminates personal judgment and manipulation by accountants.
- **Assists Auditors:** Gives auditors a baseline rulebook to verify financial statements against.

## Global Accounting Standards

### 1. IAS (International Accounting Standards)
- Created by **IASC** (International Accounting Standards Committee).
- **Principle-based.**
- *Note: These are now obsolete and have been replaced by IFRS.*

### 2. IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards)
- Created by **IASB** (International Accounting Standards Board).
- Applicable in **167+ countries**, including India.
- **Principle-based.**

### 3. US GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles)
- Created by **FASB** (Financial Accounting Standards Board) & **SEC**.
- Applicable in **USA** (Mandatory for public companies, optional for private).
- Born after the **1929 financial crisis**.
- **Rule-based.**

## IFRS vs US GAAP (Comparison)

| Feature | IFRS (International) | US GAAP (USA) |
|---------|----------------------|---------------|
| **Created By** | IASB | FASB |
| **Nature** | Principle-based | Rule-based |
| **Additional Statement** | Prepares Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow, Equity Changes, Footnotes | Prepares all IFRS statements **+ Statement of Comprehensive Income** |
| **Minority Interest** | Included in **Equity** | Included in **Liabilities** |
| **Deferred Taxes** | Shown as a **Separate Item** | Combined/Included within the related asset or liability |
| **Objectives Hierarchy** | Equal weight to Relevance, Reliability, Comparability, Understandability | Prioritizes Relevance/Reliability over Comparability/Understandability |
| **Fixed Assets Valuation** | **Fair Value** minus Depreciation | **Historical Cost** minus Depreciation |
| **Inventory Valuation** | Allows **FIFO** and **Weighted Average** only | Allows **LIFO, FIFO**, and **Weighted Average** |
| **Intangible Assets** | Recognized only if there is a **future economic benefit** | Recognized at **Fair Value** |

## Indian Accounting Standards

**Regulatory Framework:**
- Companies registered under Companies Act 2013 follow AS notified by the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)**.
- Listed companies also follow **SEBI** recommendations.

**Key Sections of Companies Act 2013:**
- **Section 134(5):** Board of Directors must ensure financial statements are mandatorily prepared as per AS.
- **Section 129(5):** If a company's financial statements deviate from AS, they must disclose:
  1. Nature of deviation
  2. Reason for deviation
  3. Financial effect of the deviation

**Evolution of Indian AS:**
- **1977:** Old Accounting Standards formulated by **ICAI** (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India) via the Accounting Standards Board (ASB). Based on Indian GAAP, not aligned with IFRS.
- **2001:** National Advisory Committee on Accounting Standards (NACAS) formed to align Indian AS with IFRS.
- **2018:** NFRA (National Financial Reporting Authority) replaced NACAS.
- **Present:** We now use **Ind AS** (Indian Accounting Standards), which are fully aligned with IFRS.
- Currently, there are **39 Ind AS** active (41 were notified, 2 withdrawn).
- Flow: ICAI recommends → NFRA reviews → MCA notifies.

> **MCQ Trap:** The old standards are just called "AS" (e.g., AS 2). The new IFRS-aligned standards are called "Ind AS" (e.g., Ind AS 2).

**Important AS / Ind AS to Remember:**
- **AS 1:** Disclosure of Accounting Policies
- **AS 2 / Ind AS 2:** Inventory Valuation
- **AS 3 / Ind AS 7:** Cash Flow Statement
- **AS 4:** Contingencies & Events occurring after Balance Sheet
- **AS 10 / Ind AS 16:** Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE)

## MCQs Solved in the Video

**Q1.** A company wants guidance regarding Property, Plant and Equipment. Which AS/Ind AS is applicable?
**Answer:** Ind AS 16 (or Old AS 10).
*Explanation:* Ind AS 16 deals with fixed assets, their valuation, and depreciation.

**Q2.** Under Section 129(5) of the Companies Act, if a company's financial statements do not comply with Accounting Standards, what must be disclosed?
A) Nature of deviation
B) Reason for deviation
C) Financial effect
D) All of the above
**Answer: D — All of the above.**

**Q3.** Which body was established under an Act of Parliament and originally framed Indian Accounting Standards through the Accounting Standards Board?
**Answer: ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India)**
*Explanation:* ICAI formed the ASB in 1977 to formulate the original Indian accounting standards.

## Quick-Fire Revision

- **AS Purpose:** Uniformity, comparability, reliability, and fraud reduction.
- **IFRS:** Created by IASB, Principle-based, followed by 167+ countries (including India).
- **US GAAP:** Created by FASB, Rule-based, followed in USA, born after 1929 crisis.
- **Inventory Methods:** IFRS allows FIFO/Weighted Average (NO LIFO). US GAAP allows LIFO, FIFO, and Weighted Average.
- **Section 129(5):** Must disclose Nature, Reason, and Financial Effect of AS deviations.
- **Old AS vs Ind AS:** Old AS made by ICAI (1977) based on Indian GAAP. New Ind AS are aligned with IFRS.
- **Current Ind AS Count:** 39 active standards.
- **Important Standards:**
  - Inventory: AS 2 / Ind AS 2
  - Cash Flow: AS 3 / Ind AS 7
  - Property, Plant, Equipment (PPE): AS 10 / Ind AS 16
