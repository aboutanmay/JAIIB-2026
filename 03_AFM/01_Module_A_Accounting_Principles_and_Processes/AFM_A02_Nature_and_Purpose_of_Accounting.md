## What is Accounting?

Accounting is a system of **recording, classifying, and summarising** data related to:
- **Economic operations** - converting inputs into outputs
- **Financial transactions** - money-related transactions

> **Accounting = Language of Business**
> It converts **business data into business information**

---

## The 3 Steps (Heart of this lecture)

### Step 1 - Record (= Bookkeeping)
- Write down every financial transaction accurately
- Must be done **objectively** = every entry must be based on a **source document** (bill / invoice / voucher / receipt)
- No source document = no recording

> **Bookkeeping = only this step.** Recording transactions for the first time is called bookkeeping.

### Step 2 - Classify
- Group similar transactions under one account
- Sales -> Sales Account | Purchases -> Purchase Account | Expenses -> Expense Account
- Purpose: So you can quickly know totals without counting each entry one by one

### Step 3 - Summarise
- Derive meaning from classified data via financial statements:
  - Trial Balance
  - Profit and Loss Account
  - Balance Sheet
  - Cash Flow Statement
- Answers: Did the business make profit or loss? What are assets and liabilities?

> **MCQ Trap:** Cash Flow Statement preparation, Profitability Analysis, Interpretation - all belong to **Summarising**, NOT bookkeeping.

---

## The Money Measurement Rule

Accounting records **only what can be measured in money.**

| Recorded | NOT Recorded |
|----------|-------------|
| Amount borrowed / owed | Honesty of employees |
| Value of assets | Hard work of employees |
| Cash balance | Team spirit in the organisation |
| Purchased goodwill | Reputation earned through service |

Why not recorded? These cannot be measured in monetary terms objectively, and there is no source document to prove the value.

---

## Internally Generated Goodwill - Classic MCQ Scenario

> A company earned a good reputation due to excellent customer service. Management wants to show this as an asset worth Rs 50 lakh in the balance sheet.

**Why is this NOT allowed?**
- Cannot be measured objectively in monetary terms
- No source document / proof for this transaction
- It was not purchased - no cost was incurred
- Only **purchased goodwill** (paid for) can be recorded as an asset

## Purpose / Benefits of Accounting

All 8 are direct MCQ points:

1. **Systematic record-keeping** - business transactions written in organised form
2. **Decision-making support** - management decides where to invest, from where to borrow, which resource to deploy
3. **Reveals financial position** - shows asset-liability status of the business
4. **Communication to stakeholders** - provides financial info to investors, shareholders, creditors, debtors, customers, employees
5. **Fulfilling legal requirements** - accounting ensures compliance with country laws
6. **True and fair view** - financial statements must be truthful; no misleading information
7. **Legal evidence** - accounting records (bills, vouchers) can be used as proof in court disputes
8. **Comparison** - compare two financial years of the same business, OR compare one business with another

## MCQs Solved in the Video

**Q1.** Management evaluating whether to purchase a new machine or continue with existing one. Which advantage does this reflect?

Options: A) Legal evidence  B) Decision-making support  C) Tax planning  D) Audit facilitation

**Answer: B**

---

**Q2.** Which activity is classified as bookkeeping rather than accounting?

Options: A) Preparation of cash flow statement  B) Profitability analysis  C) Recording purchase transactions  D) Interpretation of financial statements

**Answer: C**
Bookkeeping = recording only. Cash flow, profitability analysis, interpretation = summarising step.

---

**Q3.** Company earned good reputation through customer service. Management wants to record it as asset worth Rs 50 lakh. Why is this NOT permitted?

**Answer:** Cannot be measured objectively in monetary terms + no source document / proof.
Accounting only records what can be measured in money with documentary evidence.

## Quick-Fire Revision

- Accounting = Record + Classify + Summarise
- Bookkeeping = only the Record step (a part / subset of accounting)
- Recording must be objective = backed by source document
- Accounting = language of business (data -> information)
- Only money-measurable transactions are recorded
- Employee honesty / hard work / team spirit -> NEVER recorded
- Internally generated goodwill -> NEVER recorded (no cost paid, no proof)
- Summarising tools: Trial Balance, P&L Account, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement
- 8 benefits: Systematic records, Decision-making, Financial position, Stakeholder communication, Legal compliance, True and fair view, Legal evidence, Comparison
- Financial statements must show **true and fair view**
